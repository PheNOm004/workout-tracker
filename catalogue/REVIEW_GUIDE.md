# Catalogue review guide

`REVIEWED_COMPLETE` means an entry has all user-facing fields, clear setup and movement steps, conservative cues, and explicit stop/adjust language. It is an editorial completeness status, not medical clearance or a substitute for coaching.

For every reviewed entry:

1. Confirm the name, muscles, equipment, category, and logging type match the movement.
2. Check that setup and steps are understandable without a video and never prescribe a fixed load or forced range.
3. Keep cues observable and concise. Remove promises, diagnosis, rehabilitation claims, and pain-tolerance language.
4. Ensure mistakes describe controllable behavior rather than blaming the user.
5. Confirm an easier link preserves the main movement intent, points to an existing common entry, and is genuinely less complex.
6. Run `node scripts/validate-catalogue.mjs` before committing.

The initial 200 are product-reviewed baseline instructions. A qualified exercise professional should audit the catalogue before TimeGo markets the content as individualized training or rehabilitation guidance.
