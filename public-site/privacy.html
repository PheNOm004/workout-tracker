PHENOM / TIMEGO

Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

This policy describes the current TimeGo Android application (com.lsing.timego), developed by Phenom.

Data stored on your device

TimeGo stores exercises, routines, workout sessions, set logs, body measurements, training preferences, recommendation state, and optional movement-limitation notes locally on your device. Core workout logging does not require an account.

Backups and optional account features

The current public-foundation build does not include a configured Firebase project or live email-delivery service. Account, cloud-backup, and email-report controls are optional product surfaces and are unavailable until those services are separately configured and enabled. They are never required for local workout logging.

Exercise catalogue and recommendations

The exercise catalogue is bundled with the app and is not fetched from GitHub at runtime. Recommendations are calculated from local workout records and explicit preferences. TimeGo does not sell personal data and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs by default.

Security and retention

Network features, when enabled in a future release, will use encrypted HTTPS transport. Local information remains until you delete it, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or replace it through an explicit restore. No cloud or report data is retained by the current build.

Your choices

You can edit or reset training preferences, export or restore a local database backup, and remove local data from the app's settings. If an account feature is enabled in a later release, account deletion will be available in the app and at the public deletion page.

Children and medical use

TimeGo is a general fitness log, not a medical device and not a source of diagnosis or treatment.
