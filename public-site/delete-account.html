Account deletion

TimeGo's current public-foundation build is local-first and has no configured live account backend. No remote account or cloud workout data is created by this build.

Delete local data

In TimeGo, open Routines → Settings → Account and email reports, choose the local-data deletion option, and confirm. You can also remove all local app data through Android Settings → Apps → TimeGo → Storage.

Future account users

If a later release enables account and cloud backup, use the in-app Delete account action. It will remove the account and associated cloud data; local workout history is a separate choice and is not silently erased.

If you cannot access the app, contact foresaken0004@gmail.com from the account email address and include “TimeGo account deletion” in the subject.
