# Delete a TimeGo Account and Cloud Data

**App:** TimeGo (`com.lsing.timego`)
**Public deployment requirement:** This content must be hosted at a stable HTTPS URL before public
account creation is enabled.

## Delete inside TimeGo

Open TimeGo, go to **Routines > Settings & Preferences > Account**, choose **Delete account and cloud
data**, reauthenticate when requested, and confirm. TimeGo cancels weekly and monthly reports,
deletes account-owned cloud records, and deletes the authentication account. The app reports whether
the request completed or needs support.

Cloud-account deletion does not automatically erase the workout database remaining on that phone.
TimeGo offers a separate, explicit local-data deletion choice.

## Request deletion without the app

Use the public deletion page at https://thisphnmm.com/delete-account.html or contact
foresaken0004@gmail.com with the subject `TimeGo account deletion`. The support process
must verify control of the account email without asking for a password. Do not send workout details,
passwords, authentication tokens, or identity documents by ordinary email.

The request deletes the TimeGo authentication identity, synchronized profile, workout/routine data,
report subscriptions, and delivery metadata associated with the account. Completion is confirmed to
the verified contact address. The published page must state the normal completion time and any real
retention exception: [PUBLICATION INPUT: COMPLETION WINDOW AND CONFIRMED EXCEPTIONS].

Disabling reports alone does not require account deletion. Every report also provides cadence-specific
and all-report unsubscribe actions.
