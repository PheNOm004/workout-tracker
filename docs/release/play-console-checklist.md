# TimeGo Play Console Checklist

## Developer and application setup

- [ ] Verify the personal or organization developer profile and public contact information.
- [ ] Create/reserve `com.lsing.timego` and confirm the public developer name.
- [ ] Enrol in Play App Signing and register the upload certificate.
- [ ] Host the privacy policy and account deletion page on stable HTTPS URLs.
- [ ] Set a monitored support email.

## Store presence

- [ ] Approve title, short description, full description, category, countries, and pricing.
- [ ] Upload compliant 512px icon, 1024x500 feature graphic, and representative phone screenshots.
- [ ] Keep claims factual; do not describe recommendations as medical or guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] Complete target audience and content rating, with no unintentional child-directed presentation.

## App content and policy

- [ ] Ads declaration.
- [ ] App access and reviewer instructions for optional account/report features.
- [ ] Data Safety answers reconciled against `data-safety-matrix.md` and the exact AAB.
- [ ] Health Apps declaration for workout and fitness functionality.
- [ ] Privacy policy link in Play and in the app where sensitive/account controls are explained.
- [ ] Account deletion declaration, working in-app deletion, and public deletion URL.
- [ ] Confirm report consent, unsubscribe, retention, and email provider disclosures.

## Testing and production access

- [ ] Run repository release verification from a clean approved commit.
- [ ] Upload signed AAB to Internal testing and inspect App Bundle Explorer.
- [ ] Install from Play and test upgrade/migration, offline workout logging, backup, onboarding,
  authentication, sync consent, report preview, unsubscribe, and deletion.
- [ ] Resolve pre-launch crashes, ANRs, accessibility issues, security alerts, and policy warnings.
- [ ] If the developer account is a qualifying new personal account, complete the required closed test
  with at least 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days, then apply for production access.
- [ ] Run a staged production rollout and monitor before expanding availability.
